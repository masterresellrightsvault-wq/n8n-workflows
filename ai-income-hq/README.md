## The one thing that unblocks the library work

Drop your bulk digital-product packs (or just the top ones) into a **Google Drive folder**.
Once it's shared to your account, I read it via the Drive connector, auto-build the catalog
in `01-library/`, flag likely license type per pack, and surface the ~2–3% worth curating
into premium bundles first. **We never bulk-upload raw packs** — that kills SEO and pricing.
