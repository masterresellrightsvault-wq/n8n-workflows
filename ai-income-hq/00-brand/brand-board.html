Voice & taglines

The Anti-PLR Standard. AI income, minus the overwhelm. Done-for-you products that actually work. Buy the pack. Follow the steps. Get paid. Burned by PLR? This is the fix.

Do & don't

Do

  • Orange as the ONE bold color; charcoal + paper carry the rest.
  • Plain English, warm, one clear CTA per page.
  • Show real value — deliver the outcome we promise.
  • Disclose AI creators/tools as AI.

Don't

  • No second loud color fighting the orange.
  • No jargon, hype, or fake “get rich” claims.
  • No nudity/sexual content (protects payments + ads).
  • No raw, un-rebranded PLR — ever.
