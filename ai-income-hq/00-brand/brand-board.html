Do & don't

Do

  • Purple as the ONE bold color; gold crowns it as the premium accent.
  • Heavy uppercase display headlines; clean benefit icons.
  • Deliver the outcome we promise — that's the brand.
  • Disclose AI creators/tools as AI.

Don't

  • No paint splatter — anywhere, ever.
  • No third loud color fighting the purple + gold.
  • No jargon, hype, or fake "get rich" claims.
  • No nudity/sexual content (protects payments + ads).
