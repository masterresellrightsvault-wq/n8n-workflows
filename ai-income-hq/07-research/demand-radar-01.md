## 🔥 The 5 questions beginners ask on repeat (Question Miner)
These are asked constantly in Google **and** to AI chatbots. We answer each = we get cited.
1. **"Where do I even start with AI to make money?"** (intent: learn→do)
2. **"Do I need to be technical / know how to code?"** (fear-based — big reassurance opening)
3. **"How much can I realistically make, and how fast?"** (skeptical buyer)
4. **"Can I start with no money?"** (budget objection)
5. **"How long until I see results?"** (patience/expectation)

> **Product angle:** a $9 "Start Here" pack that answers all 5 in plain English with a 14-day
> plan. Title it in their exact words: *"How to Actually Start Making Money With AI (No Code,
> No Big Budget)."* This becomes both a product AND our top SEO/AEO page.

## 💢 The 7 gaps that make buyers rage-quit (Gap & Feedback Analyst)
Real complaints about the PLR/digital products everyone else sells — **each is our spec line:**
| Buyers HATE that... | Our version MUST... |
|---|---|
| PLR is low quality, needs tons of "fleshing out" | Ship 100% ready-to-use, zero extra work |
| Sales copy is dated, no psychological triggers | Include modern, tested sales copy that converts |
| Funnel pieces don't fit together, cart gets abandoned | Deliver a complete, matched funnel (offer→email→upsell) |
| Everything is identical/saturated, no one buys | Rebrand + differentiate every product (new cover, angle, name) |
| Can't customize to their brand | Give editable files + a customization guide |
| No results without an existing list/site | Include a "get your first 100 subscribers" starter |
| Feels like a scam to sell people | Genuinely deliver the outcome — that's the brand |

> **This table is our unfair advantage.** Competitors sell the disease; we sell the cure to their
> exact complaints. Put "did-you-get-burned-by-PLR?" language right in our sales copy.

## 📈 Trend signals worth riding now (Trend Hunter)
- **Faceless high-volume video** (automated YouTube/TikTok in a niche) — most-discussed AI method.
- **AI agents / "AI employees"** as a product category is heating up → validates our "AI Employees" line.
- **"No investment" AI hustles** — huge beginner search demand; frames our free lead magnet.

## 💰 Reality benchmark to use in our copy (credible, not hypey)
Research shows a realistic path: an email list of **1,500–2,000** in a niche + a **$37–$67**
product can hit **~$1,000 in launch week**. Beginners often see first **$250–$500**. We cite this
to set honest expectations (builds trust vs. the "get rich quick" crowd).
