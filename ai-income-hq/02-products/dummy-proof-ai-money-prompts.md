# Dummy-Proof AI Money Prompts
### 30 copy-paste prompts that turn AI into your unpaid marketing team

*Beginner-friendly. No experience needed. Paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, fill the
[brackets], hit enter.*

---

## How to use this pack (read first — 60 seconds)
1. Pick a prompt for the job you need done.
2. Replace every **[BRACKET]** with your details.
3. Paste it into your AI. If the first answer isn't perfect, reply: *"make it punchier / shorter /
   more specific."* AI gets better when you push back.
4. **Golden rule:** the more specific your brackets, the better the output. "Busy moms who want
   passive income" beats "people."

## Part 1 — Find something to sell (Prompts 1–6)

**1. Niche finder**
> Act as a digital-product strategist. I want to sell digital products but don't know my niche.
> My interests: [LIST 3]. My skills: [LIST 2]. Give me 7 profitable, beginner-friendly niches
> where digital products sell well. For each: the buyer, their #1 pain, and one product idea
> under $27. Rank by how fast a beginner could get the first sale.

**2. Winning-product research**
> You are a market researcher. In the niche [NICHE], list the 10 best-selling types of digital
> products right now (planners, prompt packs, templates, guides, etc.). For each: who buys it,
> typical price, and why it sells. Then pick the 3 easiest for a solo beginner to make.

**3. "What are they Googling" finder**
> List the top 25 questions beginners in [NICHE] type into Google and ask AI chatbots. Group
> them into 4 themes. Mark the 5 questions I could answer as a paid mini-product.

**4. Offer-stack builder**
> Build me a price ladder for [NICHE]: a free lead magnet, a $9 tripwire, a $27 core product,
> a $47–97 bundle, and a $97/mo membership idea. Name each and describe what's inside in one line.

**5. Competitor gap**
> Here's a competitor's product description: [PASTE]. Find 5 gaps or weaknesses a buyer would
> notice, and tell me how to make a better version without copying them.

**6. Bundle namer**
> I have these items: [LIST]. Package them into one irresistible bundle. Give me 5 bundle names,
> a one-sentence promise for each, and a suggested price.

## Part 2 — Create the product (Prompts 7–13)

**7. Workbook outline**
> Create a full outline for a [TOPIC] workbook aimed at [AUDIENCE]. Include a title, 6–8 sections,
> what each section teaches, and one exercise or fillable per section. Keep it action-focused.

**8. Planner pages**
> Design the page list for a [TOPIC] planner: daily, weekly, and monthly pages, plus 5 bonus
> trackers relevant to [AUDIENCE]. Describe the fields on each page.

**9. Checklist generator**
> Write a step-by-step checklist for [OUTCOME] that a total beginner could follow. 15–25 steps,
> grouped into phases, each step starting with a verb.

**10. Guide writer**
> Write a clear, friendly how-to guide: "[HOW TO ___]". Audience: [AUDIENCE]. Use plain English,
> short paragraphs, numbered steps, and a "common mistakes" box. ~1,200 words.

**11. Prompt-pack builder (make more packs to sell)**
> Create 25 high-value prompts for [AUDIENCE] to accomplish [GOAL]. Each prompt must have a
> clear role, a task, and [BRACKETS] for the user to personalize. Group into 4 categories.

**12. Lead-magnet maker**
> Create a free lead magnet for [NICHE] that's genuinely useful but leaves them wanting my paid
> product [PRODUCT]. Give me the title, the format, and the outline.

**13. Upsell idea**
> A customer just bought [PRODUCT]. Suggest 3 natural upsells and the one-line pitch for each.

## Part 3 — Sell it (Prompts 14–22)

**14. Sales-page writer**
> Write a high-converting sales page for [PRODUCT] priced at $[PRICE] for [AUDIENCE]. Include:
> a scroll-stopping headline, the problem, the promise, what's inside (bullets), who it's for,
> a guarantee, an FAQ (5 Qs), and a strong CTA. Warm, no hype, plain English.

**15. Product-title tester**
> Give me 10 title options for [PRODUCT]. Mix benefit-driven, curiosity, and number-led styles.
> Then tell me which one is strongest for cold traffic and why.

**16. Email welcome sequence**
> Write a 5-email welcome sequence for new subscribers in [NICHE]. Email 1 delivers the freebie,
> emails 2–4 build trust with quick wins, email 5 pitches [PRODUCT]. Friendly, short, one CTA each.

**17. Abandoned-cart email**
> Write a 3-email cart-recovery sequence for [PRODUCT]. Handle the top objection: [OBJECTION].

**18. Objection crusher**
> List the top 7 reasons someone in [AUDIENCE] wouldn't buy [PRODUCT], and give me a one-line
> response to each I can use in copy and DMs.

**19. Bio + link-in-bio**
> Write my social bio and link-in-bio CTA for [BRAND] that sells [PRODUCT] to [AUDIENCE]. 3 options.

**20. DM script (non-spammy)**
> Write a warm, helpful DM script for when someone comments "info" on my post about [PRODUCT].
> Lead with value, no pressure, end with a soft CTA.

**21. Affiliate pitch**
> Write a short message inviting creators in [NICHE] to promote [PRODUCT] as affiliates. Explain
> the commission [X%] and why it's easy money for them.

**22. Testimonial request**
> Write a friendly message asking a happy customer for a testimonial, making it easy by giving
> them 3 quick questions to answer.

## Part 4 — Go viral (brand-safe hooks) (Prompts 23–30)

**23. Hook machine**
> Give me 20 scroll-stopping video hooks (first 3 seconds) for [PRODUCT/NICHE]. Use pattern
> interrupts, bold claims, and curiosity gaps. Keep them clean and platform-safe. No clickbait lies.

**24. Short-form script**
> Write a 30-second TikTok/Reel script for [TOPIC] that hooks in 3 seconds, delivers one useful
> tip, and ends with a CTA to [OFFER]. Include on-screen text and a caption with 5 hashtags.

**25. Carousel builder**
> Create a 7-slide Instagram carousel teaching [TOPIC]. Slide 1 = hook, slides 2–6 = value,
> slide 7 = CTA to [OFFER]. Give slide text + a design note for each.

**26. Story sequence**
> Write a 5-frame Instagram Stories sequence that warms people up to buy [PRODUCT] today, ending
> with a poll and a swipe-up/link CTA.

**27. Content calendar**
> Build me a 7-day brand-safe content calendar for [NICHE]. Each day: platform, hook, format,
> and CTA. Mix teaching, story, and offer posts (80/20 value to pitch).

**28. Repurpose engine**
> Turn this one idea: [IDEA] into a TikTok script, an IG carousel, a Pinterest pin description,
> a tweet, and an email subject line. Keep the core message consistent.

**29. Trend rider**
> Given the trending format [DESCRIBE TREND], adapt it to promote [PRODUCT] in [NICHE] without
> being cringe. Give me 3 versions.

**30. Comment-to-customer**
> Write 10 helpful, non-salesy replies I can leave on big accounts in [NICHE] that make people
> curious enough to check my profile.
